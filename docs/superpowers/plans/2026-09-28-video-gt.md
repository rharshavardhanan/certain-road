**Architecture:** `scripts/score_video_gt.py` reads a GT CSV (`start_s,end_s,note`,
`#` comments allowed) and each model's `results/video/<stem>/<model>/summary.json`,
and writes `results/video/<stem>/gt_score.json`. The matching rule is a pure
function, tested first.

## Global constraints

- Hit: a confirmed track whose span `[first_frame, last_frame]` overlaps a GT interval, and is the first track (earliest `first_frame`) to do so. Later tracks on an already-hit interval are **duplicates**. Tracks overlapping no interval are **false alarms**.
- Defaults settled 2026-09-28: a track is in the window if its `confirmed_at` lies inside it; a track overlapping several intervals hits the earliest-starting un-hit one, so a track is at most one hit.
- Report per model: potholes hit / total, duplicate tracks, false alarms per minute, median `frames_detected` per hit.
- Caveat: one annotator, interval-only, no spatial matching.
- D081 (user, 2026-09-28): counts and run-both rule **Accepted**; "P transfers on dashcam video" is the **hypothesis** the GT scoring decides, with the firing-rate flag and D074's india_test false-alarm result beside it. The settling entry comes after scoring, on the user's go-ahead.
- GT annotated from the **raw** video, never the annotated one, and the window chosen on raw pothole content, not on model output. Flooded stretch (t≈100–110 s) excluded (user; D080).
